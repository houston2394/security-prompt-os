## How to Use

### Option 1: ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini
1. Copy any prompt from the JSON files
2. Paste into your AI assistant
3. Replace [TARGET], [PASTE CODE], etc. with your specific context
4. Get instant, expert-level responses

### Option 2: Notion
1. Import `formats/notion-import.json` into Notion
2. All prompts are organized by category
3. Search and filter instantly

### Option 3: Quick Reference
1. Open README.md for the complete list
2. Search by keyword
3. Copy and paste
